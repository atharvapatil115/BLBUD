--- SLIDE 2 ---

Introduction
Belfius Bank & Insurance is one of Belgium’s leading financial institutions. 
Originally known as Dexia Bank Belgium, it became Belfius in 2012 after restructuring during the Dexia crisis. 
The bank is owned by the Belgian State and focuses on retail banking, business banking, insurance, and public sector financing.

--- SLIDE 3 ---

Foundation & History
• Origins trace back to Crédit Communal de Belgique founded in 1860.
• Became part of Dexia Group in 1996.
• Belgian government acquired Dexia Bank Belgium in 2011.
• Rebranded as Belfius on 1 March 2012.
• Headquarters: Brussels, Belgium.
